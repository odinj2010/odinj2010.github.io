About Jonathan Roberts

The hands-on story of how a rescued desert PC, JRPGs, and networking shaped a 24-year obsession with computing.

The Wilderness Salvage (Age 13)

My journey didn't start in a pristine tech lab. When I was 13, I found an abandoned computer tower out in the desert wilderness. I brought it home, tinkered with the hardware, swapped cables, and figured out how to bring it to life running Windows 98. That hands-on curiosity ignited a fire for understanding how machines work.

RPGs, FFVII & MMORPGs

The very first RPG I ever completed was Final Fantasy VII on the original PlayStation back in 1998–1999. It holds a permanent place in my heart and directly inspired my drive to build modding tools and reverse-engineering utilities for it decades later. In 2007, I bought parts on eBay to build my own custom gaming PC from scratch, diving headfirst into MMORPGs.

Skills, Systems & Education

I took college-level Java Programming and C# Programming courses, and earned a Cisco Networking certificate through college-level courses taken in high school. I've also completed training and certifications in Google Cybersecurity, giving me a solid foundation in systems architecture, networking protocols, security fundamentals, and software creation.
